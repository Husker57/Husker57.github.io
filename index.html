Service 01

Small engine service & repair

Mowers, snowblowers, generators, tillers, pumps, and the other engines that sit through a Nebraska season and then refuse the first pull.

The work is ordinary on purpose: fuel that went stale, a carburetor that will not meter, no spark, no compression, a blade that will not hold, a winterize that was skipped. You get the machine back starting, or you get a plain answer that it will not.

  • Will not start, no power, no spark
  • Carburetor, fuel, blade, winterize
  • Drop-off or on-site by arrangement

Light welding repair & fabrication

Cracked brackets. Torn implement frames. Gates that sag. Trailer tabs. The steel that failed in the field and still has a season left in it.
